## Done

### Patterns and recipes

- [x] **Track level and humanize are separate.** Each voice has a steady `level` (0–1, 1 = natural) for the mix, and the v1.93 velocity walk is now a `humanize` multiplier on top (recipe-wide amount 0–1). Replaces the MIDI-style `velocity` 50–110/127, which drifted so far that a style's relative levels were lost within a few bars. A per-track level fader in Advanced mode can use it directly.
- [x] **Undo and redo** (Nov 2026). ↶ and ↷ beside keep evolving, plus ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z: the last 50 steps of the beat (its rows, the knobs except volume, and the style it came from). NEW BEAT, EVOLVE, RE-SLICE, a style pick, a dragged slice or a quick pattern is one step each; box taps, or turns of one knob, within 2 seconds of each other are one step; each automatic evolve is a step. Undo turns keep evolving off so the beat stays put; a new change clears redo; a new voice clears the history. Possible next: a history strip of tiny grids to jump back to any step.
- [x] **Taps are captured** (Nov 2026). Slices tapped on the waveform during SAVE 8 BARS are in the MP3 and videos: they play through the engine on a ninth channel, so the capture tapes them like any hit.
- [x] **RE-SLICE** (Nov 2026). A button beside NEW BEAT and EVOLVE: every playing row gets a slice no row is playing, no two alike, picked at random whatever the row's role (on purpose: it's for getting lucky). Rows given a slice by hand keep theirs. Choosing a style puts role-matched slices back.
- [x] **The effects rack** (Nov 2026, `src/lib/audio/fx.ts`, `src/app/FxCard.svelte`). Six one-knob effects in their own EFFECTS card, in chain order with arrows between them: CRUSH (tape saturation, then bit-crush), FILTER (low-pass left, high-pass right, off in the middle), WOBBLE (chorus, flanger, warble), ECHO (with a 1/8, dotted 1/8, 1/4 switch), SPACE (the old Space knob, unchanged) and PUMP (glue, then ducking on every beat). Each is off at 0, so the rack off sounds as before. RANDOMIZE! rolls each knob off, a little or a lot, always moving at least two clearly and now and then going wild; the reset button puts them back. Both glide as one undo step. All built-in Web Audio nodes, so a capture records knob turns and PUMP's ducking and the MP3 renders them as heard; the original voice in the MP3 skips the rack. Saved beats keep Space and the rack, and a reVox loads them. Per-row sends stay as they were: an amount rolled per row, not a control.

- [x] **Effects rack, tuned** (Nov 18): done for now. If it ever needs more: the numbers are in `knobsToParams` and the `*Settings` functions in `src/lib/audio/fx.ts`.
- [x] **Review and expand panning.** Done (Nov 2026): a PAN view beside MIX and PATTERN replaces the Pan knob. Each row is FIXED (one handle: place it anywhere left to right) or ROAM (two handles: it wanders between them, the v1.93 auto-pan ramps). A dot shows where each row is as it plays. By default rows spread like a drum kit in a mix: kick fixed in the middle, snare fixed just left, hats roaming a little right, percussion wide, tonal the middle 40%, and a second row of the same role on the other side; styles and saved beats can set a row's `pan`, and older beats saved with the Pan knob (no per-row pan) get the role defaults when reVoxed. Pan changes are undoable. Done for now (Nov 18); possible later: pan patterns tied to the beat (a hat bouncing left and right every bar), roam speed (fixed at 2–10 s per sweep), and how panning reads in the shared MP3 and video.

- [x] **Level each voice by its loudness** (Nov 2026, `src/lib/audio/prepare.ts`). A recording used to be peak-normalized, so one loud plosive or bump made the whole voice quiet. Now what's played and saved is brought to -18 dBFS RMS, measured over the voice only (frames within 30 dB of the loudest, less the loudest tenth), boosted 12 dB at most so a whisper's room noise isn't pumped up, turned down 6 dB at most, and peaks caught by a 5 ms look-ahead limiter at 0.95. Slicing still runs on the peak-normalized version, so slices fall exactly where they did. On the 2008 recordings, 80% land within 1.5 dB of each other (they spread over 18 dB before).

### Beat templates and drum roles

- [x] **Recipe format.** JSON with 16-character patterns in drum-machine notation (`x` hit, `X` accent, `o` ghost, `.` rest), per-voice role, optional slice, and tempo, swing, space and evolve settings. See `src/lib/recipe.ts`. Styles and saved beats share it.
- [x] **Guess which drum each slice suits.** Done in `src/lib/audio/drumRoles.ts`: styles deal slices by role, Evolve keeps role voices on good fits, drum roles get tighter gates, and the slicer lab shows each slice's role. Possible next: per-role tone shaping (low shelf on kicks). Original idea: Classify slices by sound and put them on matching voices in templates:
  - **kick:** low spectral centroid, energy below ~200 Hz, short (deep vowels, "b", "d", "oo");
  - **snare:** broadband and noisy mid-range with a sharp attack ("t", "k", "ch", "p");
  - **hi-hat:** high centroid, very noisy (high spectral flatness), very short (sibilants "s", "sh", "ts");
  - **tonal / other:** voiced, pitched slices for melodic or "lead" voices.

  Each slice gets scores for all roles, so a template can take the best kick, the two best hats, and so on. It could also shape slices to fit their role: shorter for hats, a low-shelf boost for kicks. Prototype in the slicer lab by showing the role per slice and letting you audition by role.
